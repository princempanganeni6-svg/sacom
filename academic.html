Academic & Research Advisory Services

SACOM’s Academic & Research Advisory division provides comprehensive, methodologically rigorous support to scholars, university students, and independent researchers navigating quantitative investigations. Founded and led by experienced statisticians, we bridge the gap between complex statistical theory and seamless academic execution, ensuring every research project meets international academic standards. All final documentation, research proposals, and statistical reports generated through our advisory services are expertly compiled using LaTeX, delivering pristine typesetting, flawless formula formatting, and exact academic alignment required by leading universities and peer-reviewed journals.

Our Full-Scope Advisory Services

Undergraduate & Postgraduate Dissertation Assistance

We offer end-to-end guidance for students completing Bachelor's dissertations and other general projects. Our team assists candidates in structuring research frameworks, formulating clear problem statements, defining testable research hypotheses, and aligning specific objectives with appropriate quantitative methodologies.

Sample Size Determination & Sampling Methodology

We design mathematically sound sampling frameworks tailored to study designs, including simple random sampling, stratified sampling, cluster sampling, and systematic sampling. Using precise power analysis calculations, we ensure your sample size provides adequate statistical power while optimizing field resource allocation.

Survey & Instrument Design

We develop structured questionnaires, Likert scales, and data collection tools tailored for academic studies. Our specialists conduct pre-testing, pilot study evaluations, and psychometric validation, including Cronbach's alpha reliability analysis and factor analysis, to guarantee high data integrity before full-scale deployment.

Data Cleaning, Screening & Processing

Raw research datasets frequently contain missing values, outliers, and formatting inconsistencies. We perform thorough data diagnostics, normality testing, missing value imputations, and structural dataset transformations to prepare data for formal modeling.

Advanced Quantitative Analysis & Statistical Software Implementation

Depending on project requirements and academic department standards, we execute precise computations across industry-standard statistical software packages, including R, SPSS and Stata. Our analytical capabilities encompass:

Descriptive & Exploratory Analysis

Cross-tabulations, summary statistics, frequency distributions, and interactive data visualizations.

Parametric & Non-Parametric Testing

Independent/paired t-tests, ANOVA, MANOVA, Mann-Whitney U, Wilcoxon signed-rank, and Chi-square tests.

Regression & Econometric Modeling

Multiple linear, binary/multinomial logistic regression, panel data, time-series (ARIMA/GARCH), and Cox hazards.

Multivariate Techniques

PCA, Exploratory & Confirmatory Factor Analysis (EFA/CFA), Cluster Analysis, and Structural Equation Modeling (SEM).

Empirical Result Interpretation & Publication-Ready LaTeX Reporting

Beyond generating output tables, we provide clear, in-depth interpretations of statistical findings, effect sizes, p-values, and confidence intervals. Every deliverable, including summary tables, statistical figures, mathematical equations, and references, is professionally rendered in LaTeX to ensure immaculate presentation, exact mathematical typography, and ready-to-submit formatting.
