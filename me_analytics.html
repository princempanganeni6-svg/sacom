Monitoring, Evaluation & Development Analytics (M&E)

SACOM’s Monitoring, Evaluation & Development Analytics division provides comprehensive, methodologically sound M&E frameworks and quantitative field evaluation solutions for non-governmental organizations (NGOs), civil society groups, international development partners, and public sector institutions. Led by qualified statisticians and research methodologists, we ensure that social interventions, public health projects, and community development programs are built upon verifiable, data-driven evidence.

Our Core Services Across the M&E and Social Development Lifecycle

M&E Framework Design & Indicator Tracking

We design robust, customized Monitoring & Evaluation systems tailored to project logic models and Theory of Change (ToC) frameworks. We define clear baseline indicators, output metrics, outcome measures, and long-term impact targets, establishing systematic tracking matrices to monitor progress across all project implementation phases.

Baseline, Midterm & Endline Evaluations

SACOM conducts independent, rigorous quantitative evaluations to assess program relevance, operational efficiency, effectiveness, sustainability, and overall social impact. Our team handles complete evaluation protocols—from designing statistically representative sampling strategies to synthesizing complex field findings into actionable, policy-relevant reports.

Digital Survey Programming & Mobile Data Collection Setup

To guarantee data integrity and eliminate transcription errors in field operations, we program, configure, and deploy digital survey instruments onto mobile platforms, including KoboToolbox, ODK (Open Data Kit), and CSPro. We integrate advanced skip logic, validation rules, geographic location tagging (GPS), and real-time field data synchronization for seamless remote monitoring.

Field Survey Operations & Quantitative Data Management

We manage full-scale quantitative data collection activities, overseeing enumerator training, field pre-testing, data quality assurance audits, and back-checking procedures. Collected data is cleaned, structured, and processed through automated validation scripts to produce secure, analysis-ready datasets.

Quantitative Impact Evaluation & Econometric Analysis

Beyond tracking basic performance indicators, we apply rigorous counterfactual methodologies and econometric modeling—such as Difference-in-Differences (DiD), Propensity Score Matching (PSM), and regression discontinuity designs using R, Stata, SPSS, and Python—to measure the true net impact of development interventions on beneficiary populations.

Interactive M&E Dashboards & Policy Reporting

We convert complex project tracking data into automated, visual Power BI and Excel dashboards to give program managers and donors real-time visibility into project indicators. All evaluation reports, executive summaries, and mathematical annexes are professionally formatted and compiled in LaTeX to meet international donor standards.
