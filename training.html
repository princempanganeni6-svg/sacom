Software Training & Professional Capacity Building

SACOM’s Software Training & Professional Capacity Building division equips students, university researchers, corporate personnel, and organization teams with practical, industry-relevant data skills. Led by qualified statisticians and experienced data analysts, our hands-on training modules bridge the gap between abstract statistical theory and real-world software application, empowering participants to clean, analyze, visualize, and interpret complex data independently.

Our Comprehensive Capacity-Building Offerings

Microsoft Excel Training (Basic to Advanced)

We offer modular training covering foundational spreadsheets up to advanced enterprise modeling. Topics include essential formulas and functions (XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, nested logical statements), dynamic Pivot Tables, data validation, conditional formatting, macro automation basics, and building executive interactive Excel dashboards.

SPSS for Survey & Statistical Analysis

Designed for academic researchers, survey analysts, and social science practitioners, this module covers the entire SPSS workflow—including dataset creation, variable definition, data cleaning, cross-tabulation, reliability testing (Cronbach’s alpha), parametric and non-parametric hypothesis testing, Analysis of Variance (ANOVA), and multiple linear and logistic regression modeling.

Advanced Statistical Computing in R & Python

Tailored for aspiring data scientists and quantitative analysts, our R and Python programs focus on modern statistical programming. Participants learn data manipulation using packages like tidyverse and pandas, custom data visualization with ggplot2 and seaborn, automated scripting, reproducible research workflows, and advanced econometric and machine learning modeling.

Stata for Econometric & Public Health Analytics

Built specifically for economists, public health professionals, and policy researchers, this course provides deep instruction in Stata programming (do-files), data management, panel data modeling, time-series analysis, survival analysis, and quantitative policy evaluation methods.

Digital Survey Tools & Mobile Data Collection

We conduct practical workshops on setting up digital mobile data collection pipelines using KoboToolbox, ODK (Open Data Kit), and CSPro. Participants learn to build digital questionnaires with complex skip logic, validation constraints, and GPS tracking, as well as manage remote enumerator deployment and real-time cloud data aggregation.

Power BI & Business Intelligence Dashboards

Aimed at corporate teams and business analysts, this course focuses on transforming raw relational databases into visual intelligence. Key areas include connecting multiple data sources, data transformation in Power Query, Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) for dynamic metrics, and designing interactive executive Power BI reports.

Flexible Learning Formats & Customized Corporate Delivery

We accommodate diverse learning needs through flexible arrangements—including one-on-one personalized tutoring for university candidates, intensive weekend masterclasses, and fully customized on-site corporate workshops tailored to an organization’s specific operational datasets and reporting requirements. Every participant receives practical datasets, step-by-step code scripts/guides, and post-training support to ensure seamless real-world application.
